# plan.ai — Departments

plan.ai organizes AI capabilities as structured departments — not generic tools. Each department operates as a defined role within the organization.

## The Six Core Departments

### Command
Synthesizes priorities, surfaces what matters, and drafts decision briefs. The strategic coordination layer.

### Analysis
Monitors markets, digests research, and scans competitors. Turns hours of research into decision-ready intelligence.

### Voice
Maintains tone consistency and drafts communications at brand-level quality across every channel.

### Growth
Builds campaign architectures, generates content systems, and maps distribution strategies.

### Build
Structures web pages, iterates UX, and proposes design improvements.

### Process
Captures procedures, maintains documentation, and handles interdepartmental handoffs. Documentation becomes a natural byproduct of work.

## How Departments Work

- Each department has defined responsibilities, inputs, and outputs
- Departments can coordinate handoffs with governance rules
- Start with one department and expand when results speak for themselves
- Organizational memory compounds over time — each department deepens integration
