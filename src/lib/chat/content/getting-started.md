# plan.ai — Getting Started & Compounding Value

## How to Start

Pick one department and one workflow — the area where your team loses the most time. Validate value in a pilot. Expand when results speak for themselves. Most organizations start with one node and one role.

## Compounding Timeline

### Week 1
Start with a single department. Immediate leverage on the highest-friction workflow in your organization.

### Month 3
The knowledge spine begins surfacing patterns. Decisions get faster as organizational memory accumulates.

### Month 6
Multiple departments can coordinate with defined triggers. Documentation becomes a byproduct of work, not a separate effort.

## Why It Compounds

- The node physically lives in your organization's space — it cannot be cancelled with a click
- Organizational memory compounds locally under your control
- Each new department deepens integration and increases system value
- Compliance-oriented governance is built into the architecture from day one

## Why Hardware Instead of SaaS?

Hardware creates control, and control creates trust. For privacy-sensitive environments, local-first infrastructure reduces exposure and simplifies governance. For many organizations, it replaces escalating monthly tool sprawl with a durable foundation that improves over time.

## How Is This Different From Running Open-Source Models?

Running models is the easy part. Orchestrating autonomous departments, maintaining security boundaries, building organizational memory, and creating reliable handoff workflows is the operating layer. plan.ai turns raw model capability into organizational infrastructure.
